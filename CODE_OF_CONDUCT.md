# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior

- **Respect**: Treat others with respect and consideration
- **Inclusivity**: Welcome and include all community members
- **Collaboration**: Work together constructively
- **Professionalism**: Maintain professional conduct
- **Helpfulness**: Assist others when possible
- **Open-mindedness**: Be open to different perspectives

### Unacceptable Behavior

- **Harassment**: Any form of harassment
- **Discrimination**: Discrimination based on protected characteristics
- **Disrespect**: Disrespectful or offensive language
- **Exclusion**: Excluding or marginalizing community members
- **Threats**: Threats of violence or harm
- **Spam**: Spamming or trolling

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please:

1. **Contact Maintainers**
   - Email: support@example.com (placeholder)
   - GitHub: https://github.com/syed-sameer-ul-hassan/FishME/issues

2. **Provide Details**
   - What happened
   - When it happened
   - Who was involved
   - Any evidence (screenshots, logs, etc.)

3. **Confidentiality**
   - Reports will be kept confidential
   - We will investigate promptly
   - We will take appropriate action

## Enforcement

### Consequences

- **Warning**: First offense
- **Temporary Suspension**: Repeated offenses
- **Permanent Ban**: Severe or repeated violations

### Appeal Process

If you believe you've been unfairly sanctioned:
1. Contact the maintainers
2. Provide your case
3. We will review and respond within 7 days

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to:
- All community spaces
- GitHub repositories
- Issues and pull requests
- Discussions and comments
- Private communications related to the project
